Privacy policy

ReRT EQD2 (Re-irradiation cumulative EQD2 calculator) · Designed by Ali Eid, MPE (Medical Physics Expert)
Effective 3 November 2026

In short: the app collects nothing. Everything you enter stays on your own device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores

Patient identifiers, dates, doses, DVH files and the case archive you create are saved only in the storage of the device and browser (or desktop app) you are using. They are not uploaded, synchronised or backed up by the app. Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling it, deletes them.

What the app sends

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party software development kits. After the first download it works without an internet connection. The only network traffic is the download of the app's own files from GitHub Pages, which hosts the app; GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP address) under its own privacy statement.

Files you export

CSV exports and PDF reports are created on your device only when you ask for them. Where they go afterwards (e-mail, hospital systems, printing) is under your control and your institution's data-protection rules.

Permissions

The Android app asks for no special permissions (no camera, location, contacts or microphone). It opens the hosted web app in a secure full-screen browser view.

Children

The app is a professional tool for radiotherapy staff and is not directed at children.

Clinical use

The app is a calculation aid for qualified professionals. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Every result must be checked independently before clinical use; consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: Ali Eid, MPE (Medical Physics Expert).
